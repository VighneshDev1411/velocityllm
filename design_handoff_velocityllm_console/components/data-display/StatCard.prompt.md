The console's signature KPI tile — every dashboard/analytics/monitoring page opens with a row of these.

```jsx
<StatCard icon={<Icon name="activity" size={18}/>} label="Total Requests" value="12.4K" subtext="4.2 req/s" color="blue" />
```

Kicker label is mono, uppercase, tracked out wide. Value is large mono. The left edge carries a 3px inset accent border in the chosen color, and the icon chip is tinted to match.
